# Dataset AH-001

## WOAH ANIMUSE

### Organization

World Organisation for Animal Health (WOAH)

---

### Evidence Layer

Veterinary Antimicrobial Use (AMU)

---

### Scientific Rationale

WOAH ANIMUSE is the global reference framework for monitoring veterinary antimicrobial use.

Unlike AMR surveillance systems, ANIMUSE measures antimicrobial exposure and selection pressure, providing a critical evidence layer for understanding resistance emergence within the One Health ecosystem.

---

### Dataset Profile

- Domain: Animal Health
- Evidence Layer: Veterinary Antimicrobial Use
- Geographic Coverage: Global
- Evidence Type: Veterinary AMU Surveillance
- Data Level: National Aggregated Reporting
- Update Frequency: Annual

---

### Scientific Strengths

- Global veterinary AMU reference framework
- Strong One Health relevance
- Internationally standardized reporting
- Supports stewardship and policy
- Long-term sustainability

---

### Scientific Weaknesses

- National aggregated reporting
- Heterogeneous reporting quality
- Sales versus use reporting differences
- Variable species resolution

---

Reusable: Excellent

Overall FAIR Maturity: High

# Dataset AH-002

## WOAH WAHIS

### Organization

World Organisation for Animal Health (WOAH)

### Evidence Layer

Animal Health Surveillance Context

### Scientific Rationale

The World Animal Health Information System (WAHIS) is WOAH's official global animal disease information platform. It provides animal disease occurrence, outbreak notifications, surveillance reports, and epidemiological context that complements AMR and antimicrobial use evidence.

### Dataset Profile

- Domain: Animal Health
- Evidence Layer: Animal Health Surveillance Context
- Geographic Coverage: Global
- Evidence Type: Animal Disease Surveillance
- Data Level: National and event-based reporting
- Update Frequency: Continuous with periodic reporting

### Scientific Strengths

- Global coverage
- Epidemiological context
- Standardized reporting
- Long-term sustainability
- Strong One Health relevance

### Scientific Weaknesses

- Not an AMR dataset
- Variable national reporting quality
- Event-oriented surveillance
- Metadata variability

### FAIR Assessment

Findable: Excellent

Accessible: Good

Interoperable: Good

Reusable: Excellent

Overall FAIR Maturity: High

### GOHIF Recommendation

Decision: Accepted

Tier: Tier 1

Evidence Layer: Animal Health Surveillance Context

# Dataset AH-003

## ESVAC

### Organization

European Medicines Agency (EMA)

### Evidence Layer

Veterinary Antimicrobial Use (AMU)

### Scientific Rationale

ESVAC provides standardized veterinary antimicrobial consumption estimates across Europe using harmonized methodologies, including population-corrected metrics, supporting stewardship and international comparison.

### Dataset Profile

- Domain: Animal Health
- Evidence Layer: Veterinary Antimicrobial Use
- Geographic Coverage: Europe
- Evidence Type: Veterinary Antimicrobial Consumption
- Data Level: National
- Update Frequency: Annual

### Scientific Strengths

- Very high standardization
- Excellent metadata
- Strong stewardship value
- Long-term surveillance
- High international credibility

### Scientific Weaknesses

- Europe only
- National aggregation
- Limited farm-level detail

# Dataset AH-004

## DANMAP

### Organization

Danish Integrated Antimicrobial Resistance Monitoring and Research Programme

### Primary Evidence Layer

Animal AMR Surveillance

### Secondary Evidence Layers

- Veterinary Antimicrobial Use
- Food Chain
- Human Health

### Scientific Rationale

DANMAP is one of the world's most mature integrated One Health surveillance programs, linking antimicrobial use and antimicrobial resistance across animals, food, and humans through harmonized national surveillance.

### Dataset Profile

- Domain: Animal Health (Cross-Domain)
- Geographic Coverage: Denmark
- Evidence Type: Integrated One Health Surveillance
- Update Frequency: Annual

### Scientific Strengths

- Integrated One Health design
- Long-term surveillance
- Harmonized methodology
- International influence
- Cross-domain integration

### Scientific Weaknesses

- National scope
- High-resource setting
- Limited global representativeness
