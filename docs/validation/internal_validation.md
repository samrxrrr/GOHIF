# GOHIF Internal Validation

Version: 1.0

Status: In Progress

---

## Purpose

Internal validation evaluates GOHIF resources using validation procedures
conducted entirely within the framework prior to external assessment.

---

## Scope

Internal validation applies to:

- datasets
- metadata
- computational workflows
- AI analyses
- benchmark studies
- governance documentation

---

## Validation Activities

Internal validation includes:

- completeness assessment
- consistency checks
- integrity verification
- reproducibility testing
- benchmark comparison
- quality review

---

## Outcomes

Internal validation may result in:

- Approved
- Approved with Minor Revisions
- Major Revision Required
- Rejected

All outcomes shall be documented and retained for audit purposes.
