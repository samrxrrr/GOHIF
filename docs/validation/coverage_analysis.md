# GOHIF Validation Coverage Analysis

Version: 1.0

Status: In Progress

---

## Purpose

Coverage analysis evaluates whether validation activities adequately assess
all governed components of GOHIF.

---

## Coverage Domains

Validation shall cover:

- datasets
- metadata
- domain registries
- computational workflows
- AI analyses
- benchmark studies
- documentation
- governance artifacts

---

## Assessment Criteria

Coverage shall be evaluated for:

- completeness
- consistency
- representativeness
- traceability
- reproducibility

---

## Review Outcome

Coverage status may be recorded as:

- Complete
- Partial
- Insufficient
