# GOHIF Statistical Validation

Version: 1.0

Status: In Progress

---

## Purpose

Statistical validation ensures that analytical conclusions generated within
GOHIF are supported by appropriate quantitative methodology.

---

## Requirements

Statistical analyses shall specify:

- study design
- sample size
- assumptions
- statistical tests
- significance thresholds
- confidence intervals
- effect sizes where applicable

---

## Reporting Standards

Every statistical report shall include:

- methodology
- software
- parameters
- reproducibility information
- interpretation
- limitations

---

## Governance

Statistical methods shall be scientifically justified and documented before
approval.
