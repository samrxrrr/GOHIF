# GOHIF Validation Framework

Version: 1.0

Status: In Progress

---

## Purpose

The Validation Framework establishes the governance principles, lifecycle, and
quality requirements governing validation activities throughout GOHIF.

Validation ensures that datasets, computational workflows, evidence
integration, AI-assisted analyses, and generated outputs satisfy predefined
scientific, technical, and governance requirements before adoption.

---

## Objectives

The framework shall ensure:

- scientific credibility
- methodological consistency
- reproducibility
- traceability
- transparency
- governance compliance

---

## Validation Scope

Validation applies to:

- datasets
- metadata
- evidence integration
- computational workflows
- AI outputs
- statistical analyses
- benchmark studies
- documentation
- governance artifacts

---

## Validation Lifecycle

1. Planning
2. Validation Design
3. Execution
4. Review
5. Corrective Actions
6. Approval
7. Freeze

---

## Governance Principles

Validation activities shall be:

- documented
- reproducible
- independently reviewable
- evidence based
- version controlled
- continuously improvable
