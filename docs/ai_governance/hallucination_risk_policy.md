# GOHIF Hallucination Risk Policy

Version: 1.0

Status: In Progress

---

## Purpose

This policy defines controls for identifying, reducing, documenting, and
reviewing hallucination risks associated with AI systems.

---

## High-Risk Outputs

Examples include:

- fabricated references
- unsupported scientific claims
- invented datasets
- incorrect metadata
- fictitious identifiers

---

## Required Controls

- human review
- evidence verification
- provenance checking
- citation validation
- reproducibility assessment

---

## Governance

Hallucinated information shall be removed before approval of any governed
output.
