# GOHIF AI Ethics

Version: 1.0

Status: In Progress

---

## Purpose

This policy defines the ethical principles governing AI use within GOHIF.

---

## Principles

GOHIF promotes:

- scientific integrity
- transparency
- accountability
- fairness
- reproducibility
- explainability
- responsible innovation

---

## Ethical Requirements

AI shall not:

- fabricate scientific evidence
- conceal uncertainty
- replace expert judgement
- misrepresent confidence
- compromise provenance

---

## Governance

Ethical considerations shall be incorporated into AI review and approval
processes.
