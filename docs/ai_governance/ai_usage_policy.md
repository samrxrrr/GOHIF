# GOHIF AI Usage Policy

Version: 1.0

Status: In Progress

---

## Purpose

This policy governs the acceptable use of artificial intelligence throughout
GOHIF.

---

## Approved Uses

AI may assist with:

- literature summarisation
- metadata enrichment
- evidence classification
- documentation
- code generation
- prioritisation
- hypothesis generation

---

## Restricted Uses

AI shall not independently:

- approve scientific evidence
- replace expert judgement
- fabricate references
- alter provenance
- overwrite governance decisions

---

## Principle

AI functions as a decision-support system rather than an autonomous scientific
authority.
