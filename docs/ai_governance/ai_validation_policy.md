# GOHIF AI Validation Policy

Version: 1.0

Status: In Progress

---

## Purpose

This policy defines validation requirements for AI-assisted outputs before
integration into GOHIF.

---

## Validation Criteria

Outputs shall be evaluated for:

- scientific correctness
- evidence traceability
- reproducibility
- explainability
- governance compliance

---

## Validation Outcomes

- Approved
- Approved with Revisions
- Deferred
- Rejected

---

## Governance

Validation records shall be retained for audit purposes.
