# GOHIF AI Audit Checklist

Version: 1.0

Status: In Progress

---

## Purpose

This checklist supports periodic governance audits of AI-assisted workflows
implemented within GOHIF.

---

## Audit Items

□ Approved AI model used

□ Model recorded in Model Registry

□ Prompt documented

□ Explainability requirements satisfied

□ Evidence traceability verified

□ Human review completed

□ Scientific validation completed

□ Citations verified

□ Provenance preserved

□ Governance approval documented

---

## Audit Outcome

Status:

- Pass
- Minor Findings
- Major Findings
- Fail

Corrective actions shall be recorded for all non-conformities.
