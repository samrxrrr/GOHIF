# GOHIF AI Version Management

Version: 1.0

Status: In Progress

---

## Purpose

This policy governs version control for AI models, prompts, workflows, and
associated governance documentation.

---

## Versioned Components

The following shall be version controlled:

- AI models
- prompts
- governance policies
- validation records
- audit reports
- workflow definitions

---

## Change Management

Every version update shall record:

- version identifier
- summary of changes
- approval date
- reviewer
- rationale

---

## Governance

Historical versions shall remain archived to preserve reproducibility and audit
traceability.
