# GOHIF Synchronization Policy

Version: 1.0

Status: In Progress

---

## Purpose

This policy governs synchronization between repository components to maintain
consistency.

---

## Synchronization Scope

The following shall remain synchronized:

- MRD
- MDD
- governance packages
- domain registry
- metadata
- validation records

---

## Verification

Synchronization checks shall be performed before major releases.

---

## Governance

Detected inconsistencies shall be corrected before repository publication.
