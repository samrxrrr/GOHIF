# GOHIF Provenance Policy

Version: 1.0

Status: In Progress

---

## Purpose

This policy defines the provenance requirements for every evidence resource
acquired within GOHIF.

Complete provenance enables scientific traceability, reproducibility, audit,
and long-term evidence integrity.

---

## Mandatory Provenance Fields

Every acquired resource shall record:

- unique resource identifier
- original source
- acquisition date
- acquisition method
- source version
- licence
- checksum (where applicable)
- responsible curator
- scientific domain
- processing history

---

## Provenance Principles

GOHIF requires provenance to be:

- complete
- immutable after approval
- version controlled
- machine readable where practical
- auditable

---

## Governance

Missing provenance prevents evidence approval until corrected.
