# GOHIF Source Catalog Policy

Version: 1.0

Status: In Progress

---

## Purpose

This policy governs how evidence sources are catalogued within GOHIF.

---

## Approved Source Categories

- International organisations
- Government repositories
- Surveillance systems
- Peer-reviewed literature
- Public biological databases
- Curated reference resources

---

## Required Metadata

Each source shall record:

- source identifier
- organisation
- URL
- licence
- update frequency
- scientific domain
- evidence class
- review status

---

## Maintenance

Source records shall be reviewed periodically to ensure continued availability,
scientific relevance, and compliance with licensing requirements.
