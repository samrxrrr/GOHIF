# GOHIF Evidence Acquisition Audit Checklist

Version: 1.0

Status: In Progress

---

## Purpose

This checklist supports governance audits of evidence acquisition activities.

---

## Acquisition Audit

□ Source identified

□ Scientific relevance verified

□ Licence reviewed

□ Metadata captured

□ Provenance recorded

□ Version documented

□ Quality screening completed

□ Validation completed

□ Governance approval recorded

□ Evidence archived

---

## Audit Outcome

Status:

- Pass
- Minor Findings
- Major Findings
- Fail

Auditors shall document corrective actions for all findings.
