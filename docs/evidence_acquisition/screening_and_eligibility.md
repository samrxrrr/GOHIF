# GOHIF Screening and Eligibility

Version: 1.0

Status: In Progress

---

## Purpose

This document defines the criteria used to determine whether candidate evidence
is suitable for inclusion within GOHIF.

---

## Inclusion Criteria

Evidence should:

- originate from authoritative sources
- be scientifically relevant
- contain sufficient metadata
- permit reproducibility
- satisfy licensing requirements

---

## Exclusion Criteria

Evidence may be excluded if it:

- lacks provenance
- cannot be verified
- is obsolete
- duplicates existing evidence
- violates licensing restrictions

---

## Outcomes

Screening decisions shall be classified as:

- Accepted
- Accepted with Conditions
- Deferred
- Rejected

All decisions shall be documented.
