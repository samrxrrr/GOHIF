# GOHIF Evidence Acquisition Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

Version: 1.0

Status: In Progress

---

## Purpose

This SOP defines the standardized operational procedure for acquiring evidence
throughout the GOHIF framework.

The procedure ensures that all evidence is obtained consistently, documented
completely, and evaluated according to governance requirements before
integration.

---

## Workflow

Step 1
Identify candidate evidence sources.

Step 2
Perform preliminary eligibility assessment.

Step 3
Verify licensing and accessibility.

Step 4
Capture metadata.

Step 5
Record provenance.

Step 6
Acquire evidence.

Step 7
Quality screening.

Step 8
Scientific review.

Step 9
Validation.

Step 10
Register within GOHIF.

---

## Required Records

Every acquisition shall include:

- acquisition date
- source organization
- resource identifier
- version
- licence
- provenance
- reviewer
- approval status

---

## Governance

All deviations from this SOP shall be documented in the governance decision log.
