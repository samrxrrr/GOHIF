# GOHIF Evidence Acquisition Framework

Version: 1.0

Status: In Progress

---

## Purpose

The Evidence Acquisition Framework defines the governance lifecycle for
identifying, evaluating, acquiring, documenting, and maintaining scientific
evidence within GOHIF.

---

## Objectives

The framework shall ensure:

- scientific quality
- reproducibility
- traceability
- transparency
- interoperability
- governance compliance

---

## Lifecycle

1. Discovery
2. Screening
3. Eligibility Assessment
4. Prioritization
5. Acquisition
6. Documentation
7. Review
8. Approval
9. Periodic Update

---

## Governance Principles

Evidence acquisition shall be:

- systematic
- reproducible
- evidence based
- fully documented
- independently reviewable
- version controlled
