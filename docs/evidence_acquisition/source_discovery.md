# GOHIF Source Discovery

Version: 1.0

Status: In Progress

---

## Purpose

Source discovery defines the standardized process used to identify candidate
datasets, repositories, databases, surveillance systems, and scientific
resources for possible inclusion in GOHIF.

---

## Candidate Sources

Evidence may originate from:

- international organizations
- public repositories
- surveillance systems
- peer-reviewed publications
- government agencies
- research consortia
- curated reference databases

---

## Discovery Criteria

Candidate sources shall be evaluated for:

- scientific relevance
- authority
- data quality
- accessibility
- update frequency
- licensing
- reproducibility

---

## Outcome

Each discovered source shall be classified as:

- Accepted
- Under Review
- Deferred
- Rejected
