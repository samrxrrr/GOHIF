# GOHIF Update and Refresh Policy

Version: 1.0

Status: In Progress

---

## Purpose

This policy defines how evidence sources are monitored and refreshed throughout
the GOHIF lifecycle.

---

## Refresh Triggers

Updates may occur following:

- new source releases
- corrected datasets
- major scientific publications
- repository restructuring
- governance decisions

---

## Refresh Procedure

1. Detect update
2. Verify authenticity
3. Record provenance
4. Validate integrity
5. Update metadata
6. Archive superseded version
7. Publish change log

---

## Governance

Every refresh shall be version controlled and documented.
