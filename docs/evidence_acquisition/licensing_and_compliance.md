# GOHIF Licensing and Compliance Policy

Version: 1.0

Status: In Progress

---

## Purpose

This policy governs the legal and ethical use of evidence integrated into
GOHIF.

---

## Requirements

Evidence shall only be incorporated when:

- licence permits intended use
- attribution requirements are satisfied
- redistribution restrictions are documented
- applicable privacy and ethical requirements are respected

---

## Compliance Review

Every acquisition shall document:

- licence type
- usage restrictions
- attribution requirements
- redistribution permissions
- reviewer

---

## Governance

Resources failing compliance review shall not be incorporated into GOHIF.
