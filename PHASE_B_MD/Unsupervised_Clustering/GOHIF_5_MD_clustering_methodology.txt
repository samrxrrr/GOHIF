MD unsupervised clustering methodology
======================================
Input: 5 validated 100 ns MD candidates.
Feature source: GOHIF_5_CANDIDATE_MD_FEATURE_MATRIX.tsv
Feature panel: 12 interpretable non-constant features.
Scaling: StandardScaler (z-score).
Distance: Euclidean distance in standardized feature space.
Algorithms evaluated: KMeans and Agglomerative Ward.
Primary descriptive partition: k=2.
KMeans silhouette: 0.373957
Agglomerative Ward silhouette: 0.373957
Both methods produced the same k=2 partition.
Cluster 1: GOHIF-010_kamB, GOHIF-064_tetX6, GOHIF-179_tetX6, GOHIF-235_tetX6
Cluster 2: GOHIF-234_sgm
Interpretation limitation: n=5; clustering is exploratory and not a generalizable statistical model.
Formal protein-ligand H-bond counts were not available and were not substituted into the feature panel.
