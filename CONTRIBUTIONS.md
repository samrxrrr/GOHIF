# GOHIF Contribution Register

**Project:** Global One Health Intelligence Framework (GOHIF)

**Version:** v1.0

**Status:** Active

---

# Purpose

This document records all significant contributions made to the GOHIF project throughout its lifetime.

The purpose of this register is to ensure transparency, accountability, reproducibility, and fair attribution of work.

This document supports—but does not replace—formal manuscript authorship decisions.

---

# Contribution Principles

GOHIF follows these principles:

* Every significant contribution is documented.
* Every contribution is traceable.
* Every contributor receives appropriate credit.
* Contributions are linked to milestones and Git history.
* Scientific integrity takes precedence over contribution quantity.

---

# Contributor Roles

## Principal Investigator (PI)

Primary responsibilities include:

* Scientific leadership
* Research vision
* Framework architecture
* Software architecture
* Repository management
* Scientific review
* Engineering review
* Final approval
* Release management
* Publication leadership

---

## Research Collaborator

Responsibilities may include:

* Literature review
* Biological interpretation
* Documentation
* Assigned software modules
* Validation
* Manuscript preparation
* Figure preparation
* Data quality review

Responsibilities may expand as the project evolves.
