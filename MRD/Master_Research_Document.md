# 1. Executive Summary

The Global One Health Intelligence Framework (GOHIF) is a modular, explainable, reproducible computational framework designed to support One Health antimicrobial resistance research.

Unlike conventional AMR analysis pipelines, GOHIF focuses on creating reusable scientific infrastructure rather than isolated analyses.

The framework integrates software engineering, computational biology, governance, documentation, validation, and publication planning into a unified research platform.
