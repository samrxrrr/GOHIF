# Global One Health Intelligence Framework (GOHIF)

## An Explainable Evidence-Centric Platform for One Health Antimicrobial Resistance Intelligence

---

## Project Overview

The Global One Health Intelligence Framework (GOHIF) is a reproducible computational research framework designed to integrate heterogeneous antimicrobial resistance (AMR) evidence across the complete One Health ecosystem.

Rather than functioning as another surveillance database, GOHIF serves as an evidence integration framework that preserves provenance, evaluates evidence quality, harmonizes heterogeneous datasets, computes evidence confidence, and supports transparent hypothesis generation and decision support.

---

## Research Vision

Develop a reproducible, explainable, evidence-driven computational framework capable of integrating multi-domain One Health evidence for antimicrobial resistance surveillance, prioritization, and scientific discovery.
