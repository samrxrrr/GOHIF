GOHIF-235 × tet(X6)
FINAL 1 µs MOLECULAR DYNAMICS INTERPRETATION
============================================================

1. SIMULATION
The selected GOHIF-235 × tet(X6) complex completed a 1 µs
production MD simulation consisting of 500,000,000 steps at
0.002 ps timestep, producing 100,001 frames over 1,000,000 ps.

The production system contained 71,720 atoms and used OPC water.
GPU acceleration was used for nonbonded/PP, PME, and bonded work,
while coordinate update/constraints remained on CPU because of
virtual-site compatibility.

4. CONFORMATIONAL CLUSTERING
Clustering was performed on a 100-ps sampled Cα trajectory
containing 10,001 frames spanning the complete 1 µs simulation.

Cluster 1: 8999 frames (89.98%)
Cluster 2:  556 frames (5.56%)

Cluster 9:    3 frames (0.03%)
Cluster 10:   1 frame  (0.01%)

At 0.20 nm, the same sampled trajectory produced one cluster
containing all 10,001 frames.

The clustering therefore identifies one dominant conformational
population at the 0.15 nm exploratory resolution, with smaller
subpopulations representing a minority of sampled conformations.

5. PROTEIN-LIGAND CONTACTS
The standardized 100 ns analysis showed a protein-ligand minimum
distance ≤0.30 nm in 100% of frames.

This is a contact-persistence metric and must not be interpreted
as a formal hydrogen-bond count.

6. HYDROGEN-BOND QUALIFICATION
Formal ligand-protein hydrogen-bond counting was not accepted
because the GROMACS hydrogen-bond analysis did not correctly
recognize the ligand donor/acceptor chemistry.

Geometry-based contact/proximity measurements were retained as
the validated alternative.

7. MMPBSA
MMPBSA was explicitly excluded from the final project analysis
plan and is not part of the final evidence chain.

8. OVERALL EVIDENCE STRUCTURE
The final evidence chain is:

Docking
  ->
100 ns multi-category QC
  ->
single-candidate selection
  ->
validated 1 µs production MD
  ->
RMSD / RMSF / Rg / SASA
  ->
protein-ligand contact analysis
  ->
PCA
  ->
DCCM
  ->
conformational clustering
  ->
integrated interpretation

No claim of binding free energy from MMPBSA is made.
No formal hydrogen-bond count is reported.
The clustering result is based on Cα RMSD and 100-ps sampling.
