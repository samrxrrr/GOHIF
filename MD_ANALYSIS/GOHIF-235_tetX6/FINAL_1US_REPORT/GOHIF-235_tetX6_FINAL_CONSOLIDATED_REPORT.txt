============================================================
1. DOCKING CAMPAIGN
============================================================

Docking campaign:
40 receptors × 204 ligands = 8,160 docking pairs.

Production docking used the corrected AutoDock-GPU workflow.
Docking QC, filtering, interaction/pose assessment, ADME analysis,
integrated ranking, and the frozen Top-25 candidate set were completed.

GOHIF-235 × tet(X6) was retained as a high-priority candidate
for molecular-dynamics validation.

GOHIF-033 × IMP-13 was not taken forward to MD because its
docked pose could not be transferred into a chemically/topologically
validated MD representation without forcing an unreliable mapping.

Frozen docking artifacts were preserved.

============================================================
10. HYDROGEN-BOND QUALIFICATION
============================================================

Formal protein-ligand hydrogen-bond counts are not reported.

The GROMACS hydrogen-bond analysis did not correctly recognize
the ligand donor/acceptor chemistry despite the presence of
explicit ligand heteroatoms and topology entries.

Protein-ligand minimum-distance and contact-persistence metrics
were therefore retained as validated geometric evidence.

These metrics must not be described as hydrogen-bond counts.

============================================================
11. MMPBSA
============================================================

MMPBSA was explicitly skipped by project decision.

No MMPBSA binding free-energy value is included in the final
evidence chain.

============================================================
12. MD25 / DOCKED-POSE TRANSFER QUALIFICATION
============================================================

The authoritative tet(X6) docking pose was investigated for
transfer into the clean ligand topology.

Although coordinate correspondences could be established for
part of the structure, authoritative PDBQT branch connectivity
was not completely preserved in the clean topology.

The mapping therefore failed the required topology-provenance
validation.

The docked pose was not forcibly transferred into the final
MD topology.

Frozen docking data were not modified.
